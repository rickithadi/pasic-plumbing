## What We Did Differently

### 1. Your two markets, front and center
The Wix site leads with a single phone number. Our version shows both AZ and IL numbers in the hero — so Phoenix Valley customers and Chicagoland customers both see themselves immediately.

### 2. Commercial credibility, visible by name
Arizona Biltmore Resort. ALDI. White Castle. Navy Federal Union. These are real proof points that competitors can't fake — and the current site buries them in a portfolio no one finds. We put them front and center in their own section, numbered like a track record.

### 3. A site that actually converts
Primary action (call) is reachable in one click from every page, including the navbar. Secondary action (contact form) has AZ + IL number as fallback. The whole structure is designed around the one thing a plumbing site needs to do: get someone to pick up the phone.

### 4. SEO built in from day one
The current Wix site has no structured data, no sitemap, no meta descriptions. We added:
- JSON-LD schema (Google Plumber type — shows in search results)
- `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`
- Full Open Graph meta tags for social sharing
- Semantic HTML with proper heading structure

We reviewed Benjamin Franklin Plumbing and Bonney while designing this. Both rely on generic franchise aesthetics — blue/white, icon grids, corporate feel. The approach we took differentiates on warmth, credibility, and the personal relationship with Duke. No competitor is doing that.
